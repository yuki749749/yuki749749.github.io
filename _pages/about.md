I am a research engineer at the Medical Imaging Physics Group at KTH, under the supervision of Mats Danielsson and Mats Persson.

My research interests include deep generative models, probabilistic modeling, and statistical machine learning, along with their applications to medical and environmental domains.

I recently obtained a Master's degree in Applied and Computational Mathematics at KTH, where Soon Hoe Lim supervised my thesis. Prior to that, I received a bachelor's degree in Systems Innovation at The University of Tokyo, where Toru Sato supervised my thesis.
